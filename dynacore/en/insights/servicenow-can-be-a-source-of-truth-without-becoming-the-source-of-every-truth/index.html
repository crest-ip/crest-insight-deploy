Where ARC complements ServiceNow

Part 1 of 5

ServiceNow can be a source of truth without becoming the source of every truth

Choosing ServiceNow as a strategic platform is a rational attempt to reduce fragmentation. Shared data, workflows and controls can connect work that otherwise disappears between organizational boundaries. ServiceNow’s Enterprise Architecture proposition extends that logic into applications, data, processes and business capabilities.

The difficult question begins after the platform choice has been made: what should ServiceNow be authoritative for?

This is not a question about whether ServiceNow can represent or connect to a particular record. An extensible platform with broad integration capabilities can work with many kinds of enterprise information. It is a question about where the meaning, lifecycle and accountability for that information legitimately belong.

Authority should follow responsibility

A ServiceNow record can be the authoritative truth for an operational workflow, service state or portfolio decision managed in the platform. That authority is valuable because it is connected to the people, controls and actions that maintain the record.

Other knowledge may remain authoritative elsewhere. A specialist architecture repository may contain model semantics that are not preserved in a simplified application record. An API catalogue may own interface contracts. A policy repository may own the approved wording and review history. A person may remain accountable for an unresolved interpretation that no system can yet confirm.

Representing or synchronizing these facts in one platform does not automatically transfer their authority. It may only create another useful representation of them.

The architecture task is therefore to make authority explicit:

  • Which source owns this statement?
  • Which system holds a useful representation of it?
  • How current is that representation?
  • Who resolves a conflict?
  • Which decision is it trusted to support?

These questions strengthen the ServiceNow investment. They prevent a shared model from accumulating silent assumptions that later become difficult to distinguish from established facts.

Connect truths without flattening them

ARC starts from the environment the organization already has. In a ServiceNow organization, that means treating ServiceNow as an important source rather than asking the organization to move to another universal platform.

ServiceNow already provides extensive capabilities for connecting and working with information across enterprise systems. ARC addresses a more specific architecture problem: how to preserve and relate the additional context required for a change when that context remains authoritative across specialist repositories, documents, interfaces and accountable people.

For a particular decision, ARC can connect selected ServiceNow information with this additional architecture context. The resulting context retains its provenance, respects relevant access rules and distinguishes between established knowledge, interpretations and conclusions that still require review. Conflicts remain visible for resolution rather than being hidden by whichever representation or synchronization happened most recently.

The result is not one source of every truth. It is a governed way to relate the truths that matter for the decision at hand.

This distinction becomes more important as ServiceNow expands across strategy, portfolio, operations and execution. The more useful the platform becomes, the more decisions will cross its boundary. Those decisions may need context from inside and outside the platform without making either side less authoritative.

ServiceNow can be central without being universal. ARC helps make that boundary deliberate — and turns it from a limitation into an architectural strength.
